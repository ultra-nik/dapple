Privacy Policy — dapple

Effective date: 6 October 2026
App: dapple: Paint by Number (the "app"), for iPad
Provider: Nikita Tuganov, United Kingdom ("we", "us")
Contact: dapple.liveapp@gmail.com

The short version

dapple does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your progress and your paintings stay on your iPad. We have no servers that receive them.

What stays on your device

  • Your painting progress, finished paintings, brush and canvas settings and in-app preferences are stored locally on your iPad.
  • We cannot see this data and it is never sent to us or to any third party by the app.
  • If you back up your iPad (for example with iCloud Backup), your device backup may include the app's data. That backup is handled by Apple under Apple's own terms, not by us.
  • If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. We cannot restore it, because we do not have a copy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software from third parties.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not show ads.
  • The app has no sign-in and no sync between devices.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions (the catalog subscription and one-time painting packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, your Apple Account details or your name. The app reads only the status of your transactions from Apple's StoreKit framework on your device (for example, whether a subscription is active, and which packs you own), so it can unlock the right content. Apple's handling of your data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

If Family Sharing is on for a purchase, Apple shares the purchase with your family group. We do not see who is in your family group.

Notifications

If you start the free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules one local notification on your iPad that reminds you before the trial ends. It is created and shown by your device. It does not use a server, and no data about it leaves your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in iPadOS Settings.

Sharing your paintings

You can export a painting as an image. The export opens the system Share sheet, and the image goes only where you choose to send it (for example Files, Messages, Mail or a printer). We do not receive a copy. What happens to the image after you share it depends on the app or service you chose.

Children

dapple is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The app does not collect personal data at all, so there is nothing about a child to collect or to delete. If you believe a child has sent personal data to us by other means (for example by e-mail), contact dapple.liveapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

The controller of any personal data described in this policy is Nikita Tuganov, United Kingdom. The UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 apply to us. If you are in the EU/EEA, the EU GDPR gives you equivalent rights.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to complain to a supervisory authority.

Because the app does not collect personal data and we hold no data about you, in practice there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data is on your device and you control it: you can delete it at any time by deleting the app.

If you write to us (see "Contact"), we process your e-mail address and your message only to reply, based on our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) UK GDPR / EU GDPR). We keep the correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request, and we do not share it with anyone except our e-mail provider. Where that provider is outside the UK or the EEA, it relies on a transfer mechanism recognised by UK or EU law.

Complaints. In the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk. If you are in the EU/EEA, you can complain to your local data-protection authority; a list is on the European Data Protection Board website: https://edpb.europa.eu.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy before the change ships and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
