dapple — Support

Need a hand? Write to dapple.liveapp@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within 3 business days. Please include your iPad model, iPadOS version and the app version (Settings → About dapple → Version).

Restore purchases

Purchases are tied to your Apple Account. If a subscription or pack looks missing, for example after reinstalling the app or setting up a new iPad:

  1. Sign in to the same Apple Account you bought with (iPad Settings → your name).
  2. Open dapple → Settings → Restore purchases.
  3. If nothing appears, check Settings → Manage subscription to see whether the subscription is still active.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. To cancel:

  • In dapple: Settings → Manage subscription, or
  • On your iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → dapple.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, or it renews. After cancelling you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments, so refund requests go to Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

Move your progress to a new iPad

dapple has no accounts and no sync. Your progress and paintings are stored only on your iPad. If you set up a new iPad from an iPad backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), your progress may come over with it, because it restores the app's data. If you start fresh, progress does not transfer. Purchases are not affected: restore them with Settings → Restore purchases.

Before you wipe or replace an iPad, export the paintings you want to keep (finished painting → Share your painting).

FAQ

Is dapple free? Part of the catalog is free. All brushes and tools are free for everyone. The rest of the catalog needs a subscription or a pack purchase.

Is there a free trial? Yes, 7 days, on the Annual plan only, for eligible new subscribers. Apple decides eligibility, and it is available once per Apple Account.

Will you remind me before the trial ends? If you allow notifications, yes: a local notification, a day before the trial ends. If notifications are off, there is no reminder; cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

Does dapple collect my data? No. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Where do the paintings come from? The paintings in dapple are AI-generated and curated by hand. The brushwork is all yours.

How do I export my painting? Open the finished painting and tap Share your painting. You get a full-resolution PNG through the system Share sheet. Exported paintings are for your personal, non-commercial use (see Terms of Use).

I deleted the app. Is my progress gone? Yes, unless your device backup restores it. We have no copy. Purchases can always be restored.

Can I use it on iPhone? dapple is made for iPad and is not offered for iPhone.

Family Sharing? If Family Sharing is on for your subscription or a pack, members of your family group get access as Apple allows.
